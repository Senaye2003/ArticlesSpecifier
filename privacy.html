Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 9, 2026

Hi, I'm Senaye, and I run News Categories (newscategories.netlify.app). I built this site to help people see news from different angles, not to collect your data. Here's the honest rundown of what happens with your information when you use it.

What I collect (very little)

Newsletter signups: If you subscribe to the newsletter, your email address is stored so I can send you updates. That's the only thing it's used for.

Contact messages: If you send a message through the contact form, I receive your name, email, and message so I can reply. These go through Netlify Forms, the service that hosts this site.

Archived articles: When you archive an article, that list is saved in your own browser's local storage. It never leaves your device, and I can't see it.

What I don't do

I don't sell your information, share it with advertisers, or use tracking cookies. There are no analytics scripts profiling you while you read.

Where the articles come from

The headlines and summaries on this site are fetched from NewsAPI.org. When you click "Read more," you leave this site and the destination site's own privacy policy applies.

Hosting

This site is hosted on Netlify, which (like every web host) automatically logs basic technical details such as IP addresses when pages are served. You can read more in Netlify's privacy policy.

Your choices

Want your email removed from the newsletter list, or have any other privacy question? Just ask through the contact form and I'll take care of it.
